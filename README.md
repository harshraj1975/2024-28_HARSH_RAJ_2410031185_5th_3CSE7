# Work Completed During Internship

### 1. Social Media Data Collection and Cleaning

The internship work included understanding the collection and preparation of social media campaign data.

The data preparation process involved:

- Collecting relevant campaign data.
- Organizing data into structured datasets.
- Identifying duplicate records.
- Handling missing values.
- Checking data consistency.
- Preparing cleaned data for further analysis.

### 2. Digital Marketing Data Analysis

The cleaned data was used to understand campaign performance and audience engagement.

The analysis focused on:

- Campaign performance
- Audience engagement
- Reach
- Interactions
- Click-related metrics
- Conversion-related metrics
- ROI

### 3. KPI Reporting

Important digital marketing KPIs were studied and used for performance reporting.

Key KPIs include:

- **Reach** – Number of people who were exposed to campaign content.
- **Engagement** – User interactions such as likes, comments and shares.
- **CTR** – Measures how frequently users click on campaign links.
- **Conversion Rate** – Measures the percentage of users completing the desired action.
- **ROI** – Helps evaluate the effectiveness of marketing investment.

### 4. Strategic Recommendations

Based on campaign performance and KPI analysis, recommendations were prepared to improve digital marketing performance.
